# Community expectations

This is a learning project. Welcome beginners, respect different backgrounds, and explain corrections with patience. Critique ideas and code without attacking people. Do not harass others, share personal information without consent, post hateful content, or pressure people to disclose credentials or private work.

Keep discussions relevant and acknowledge sources. State uncertainty honestly and help others reproduce findings.

Maintainers may remove harmful content or restrict participation to protect the community. Report ordinary moderation concerns through the repository’s available contact options. For sensitive concerns, use a private maintainer contact if one is listed; avoid posting personal details publicly.
